Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9 September 2026

About this privacy policy

This Privacy Policy explains how EnergyCalc UK handles information when you use our website, calculators and guides.

We aim to collect only the information necessary to operate, understand and improve the website.

Calculator inputs

EnergyCalc UK calculators perform calculations directly in your browser.

Values you enter into calculator fields, such as electricity rates, appliance energy usage or usage frequency, are not intentionally stored by EnergyCalc UK or sent to us as part of the calculation.

The website currently does not require visitors to create an account in order to use the calculators.

Google Analytics

We use Google Analytics to understand how visitors use EnergyCalc UK and to help us improve the website.

Google Analytics may collect information such as the number of visitors, sessions, approximate geographic location, browser and device information, pages visited and interactions with the website.

Google Analytics may use first-party cookies such as _ga to distinguish visitors and sessions when analytics storage has been permitted.

We do not intentionally send names, email addresses or calculator input values to Google Analytics.

Cookies and consent

EnergyCalc UK uses CookieYes to manage cookie consent.

When you first visit the website, you may be shown a cookie consent banner allowing you to accept, reject or manage non-essential cookies.

Non-essential analytics and advertising storage are denied by default until the relevant consent choice is provided where applicable.

You can change or withdraw your cookie choices through the cookie settings control available on the website.

Consent records

CookieYes may record information about your cookie consent choice, such as whether consent was accepted or rejected, the date and time of the choice and a consent identifier. This helps maintain a record of consent preferences.

How we use information

Information collected through analytics may be used to:

  • Understand how many people visit the website.
  • Understand which calculators and guides are most useful.
  • Understand general device and browser usage.
  • Identify website performance or usability problems.
  • Improve the content and functionality of EnergyCalc UK.

Third-party services

EnergyCalc UK currently uses third-party services including Google Analytics and CookieYes.

These providers may process information according to their own privacy policies and applicable data protection requirements.

External links

EnergyCalc UK may link to external websites, including organisations and information sources such as Ofgem.

We are not responsible for the privacy practices, content or security of external websites. You should review their privacy policies when visiting them.

Data retention

Analytics and consent information may be retained according to the retention settings and policies of the services we use.

We may adjust these settings as the website develops.

Your privacy choices

Depending on where you live, you may have rights regarding your personal information, including rights to request access, correction or deletion of certain information.

You can also reject non-essential cookies or change your cookie preferences using the consent controls provided on the website.

Contact us

If you have a question about this Privacy Policy or how EnergyCalc UK handles information, contact us at:

Email: hello@ukenergycalc.co.uk

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when our website, analytics, advertising or other services change.

When we make changes, the updated date at the top of this page will be changed accordingly.
