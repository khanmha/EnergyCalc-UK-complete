How much does an electric heater cost to run?

At 26.32p/kWh, a 1kW heater costs about 26.32p per hour; 1.5kW costs 39.48p; 2kW costs 52.64p; 2.5kW costs 65.80p; and 3kW costs 78.96p per hour.

These figures assume continuous full-power operation. Use the free calculator below for your heater, tariff and daily or monthly usage.

How is the cost calculated?

The calculator converts watts to kilowatts and multiplies that by the hours used. This gives estimated electricity consumption in kWh.

Daily kWh = (Watts ÷ 1,000) × Hours used
Daily cost = Daily kWh × Electricity price

If the device's power draw changes during use, a measured average wattage will usually give a better estimate than its maximum rating.

2kW heater: daily and monthly cost examples

These are maximum/full-power examples at 26.32p/kWh, assuming the heater draws 2kW throughout:

  • 4 hours: 8kWh, costing about £2.11.

  • 4 hours per day for 30 days: 240kWh, costing about £63.17.

Totals use unrounded costs before rounding to the nearest penny. Your tariff and thermostat cycling may change the result.

How a heater thermostat affects electricity usage

A thermostatic heater may switch its heating element on and off after reaching the desired room temperature. Time switched on at the socket can therefore be longer than time drawing full power. Actual consumption may be lower than maximum wattage multiplied by total time switched on.

For example, a 2kW element heating for half of a four-hour period uses roughly 4kWh rather than 8kWh, excluding any small additional controls or fan consumption. This is an illustration, not an assumed thermostat saving: room heat loss, the temperature setting and heater controls determine runtime.

For a better estimate, use measured average power over a representative period with the full hours switched on, or rated power with the element's actual heating hours. Avoid applying both adjustments to the same usage.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does a 2kW heater cost to run for one hour?

At 26.32p/kWh, a 2kW heater running continuously at full power uses 2kWh in one hour and costs 52.64p (about £0.53).

How much does a 1kW heater cost per hour?

A 1kW heater costs 26.32p per hour at 26.32p/kWh when running continuously at full power.

How much does a 3kW heater cost per hour?

A 3kW heater costs 78.96p per hour at 26.32p/kWh when running continuously at full power.

How much does an electric heater cost to run for 8 hours?

At 26.32p/kWh and continuous full power, 8 hours costs about £2.11 for a 1kW heater, £4.21 for a 2kW heater or £6.32 for a 3kW heater. Thermostat cycling can reduce actual use.

Does a thermostat reduce electricity use?

A thermostat can reduce electricity use by switching the heating element off once the room reaches the set temperature. Consumption depends on how long the element actually runs, the temperature setting and heat loss from the room.

Are oil-filled radiators cheaper to run than fan heaters?

Electricity cost depends on actual energy consumed: at the same tariff, both cost the same for each kWh used. Heater design, thermostat behaviour and how effectively the room retains heat affect real-world usage. An oil-filled radiator does not automatically cost less simply because of its type.

Does heater wattage affect running cost?

Yes. At the same tariff, a heater drawing 2kW continuously costs twice as much per hour as one drawing 1kW. Total cost also depends on runtime and thermostat cycling; rated wattage alone does not determine the daily bill.
