Tumble Dryer Cost Per Cycle UK: Heat Pump vs Condenser

Compare the electricity cost of a complete drying load, then work out whether a more efficient dryer is worth the extra purchase price.

Quick answer

A dryer using 1.5kWh per load costs 39.5p at 26.32p/kWh, while a load using 3kWh costs 79.0p. These are illustrative inputs, not typical readings for particular dryer types. A heat pump design can reduce energy consumption, but compare individual models on the same programme and capacity.

How to read a dryer energy label

If the label reports kWh per 100 cycles, divide that number by 100. For example, 110kWh per 100 cycles means 1.10kWh per stated test cycle, costing 29.0p. Check whether two labels use the same test programme and load capacity before comparing them. Older labels may show annual consumption based on standard assumptions instead.

Cost per cycle = cycle kWh × tariff in p/kWh

Heat pump, condenser and vented dryers

Heat pump models recover heat within the drying circuit and can use less electricity for a comparable load. Conventional condenser dryers collect moisture in a container or drain; vented dryers send moist air outside. Programme times, room temperature, capacity and moisture remaining after washing all matter. Compare actual models rather than assigning a fixed cost to a dryer type.

Is paying more for a heat pump dryer worth it?

Suppose two otherwise suitable models cost £300 and £500. Assume their energy figures for equivalent loads are 3.0 and 1.2kWh respectively. At three cycles per week, the difference is 1.8 × 156 = 280.8kWh per year.

At the reference tariff, that is an estimated £73.91 annual saving. The illustrative £200 price difference takes about 2.7 years to recover from electricity alone. At one load a week it takes about 8.1 years; at five loads, about 1.6 years.

Simple payback (years) = additional purchase cost ÷ annual electricity saving

This is not a recommendation to replace a working appliance: installation, repair costs, lifespan and changing tariffs can alter the decision. When replacing a working dryer, include the entire net replacement cost, not just the difference between two new models.

Reduce the cost per completed load

Use an appropriate washing-machine spin programme, clean filters as instructed, avoid overloading and choose a drying programme that suits the fabric. If it is practical to air dry some laundry, fewer machine cycles reduce dryer electricity use. For a whole-laundry comparison include the washing machine's energy as well.

Frequently asked questions

How much does a 2kWh drying cycle cost?

52.64p at the stated tariff, excluding standing charges.

Does a longer dryer cycle cost more?

Not necessarily; a longer cycle with lower average power can consume less total energy.

Is a heat pump dryer always worth buying?

No. Compare the price difference, expected use, actual programme consumption and lifetime.

Can I use maximum wattage to calculate a cycle?

Yes as a continuous-power estimate, but metered kWh or programme data is usually more representative.
