2kW Heater Running Cost UK: Hourly Cost and Thermostat Examples

Understand what a 2kW heater can cost at full power, and estimate the difference when its heating element cycles on and off.

Quick answer

A 2kW electric heater running at full power uses 2kWh in one hour and costs 52.64p at 26.32p/kWh. Four hours of continuous heating costs £2.11; if the element heats for only half of that period, the illustrative energy cost falls to £1.05. Actual thermostat behaviour is not a fixed percentage.

Examples use 26.32p/kWh, the Ofgem average unit rate for a standard variable electricity tariff paid by Direct Debit in Great Britain. Regional and individual tariffs vary. Check your bill or our UK electricity unit rate guide.

What does 2kW mean on the bill?

2kW means a power draw of 2,000 watts while the heater is operating at that setting. It does not mean it uses 2kWh regardless of runtime.

Estimate thermostat cycling

Duty cycle means the fraction of time the heating element is actually on. A thermostat may reduce heating time after reaching the set temperature, depending on insulation, outdoor temperature, room size and draughts. The table models four hours switched on every day for 30 days.

Budget for a colder month

Choose a plausible range rather than one precise forecast. For example, if you expect four hours of daily use and the element runs between half and all of that time, the 30-day illustrative range is £31.58–£63.17. Change the rate if your tariff is different, and use the same assumptions when comparing another heater.

Does an oil-filled radiator cost less than a fan heater?

Both resistive electric heaters cost the same per kWh at the same tariff. Their heat distribution, controls and ability to maintain comfort may affect how long they need to run. Comparing two nameplate wattages alone is not a reliable comparison of the electricity required to keep a room comfortable.

Measure and use safely

Use the heater and its thermostat according to the manufacturer's instructions. Do not use an extension lead or plug-in meter unless explicitly suitable for the appliance's load and permitted by its instructions. For hardwired or high-load devices, use suitable whole-home energy readings or seek professional advice. Keep heaters away from combustible materials and never cover them.

Frequently asked questions

How much does a 2kW heater cost for 8 hours?

About £4.21 if it continuously draws 2kW at the reference tariff.

How much does a 2kW heater cost for 30 days?

At four hours per day and full power, about £63.17. Actual thermostat operation may reduce the heating energy.

Can I use the 1kW setting to halve the bill?

It halves the full-power cost per hour relative to 2kW, but the runtime needed for the same comfort may differ.

Can I estimate from my smart meter?

You can compare readings over similar periods, allowing for other appliances. A whole-home reading includes more than the heater.

Methodology and further reading

The examples use transparent assumptions, not independently measured appliance or vehicle performance. Costs are calculated before rounding, and exclude standing charges unless explicitly stated. See Ofgem's unit rates, our energy-use guide and appliance cost comparison guide.
