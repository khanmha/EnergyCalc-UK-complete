Dishwasher Cost Per Cycle UK: Electricity and Water Costs

Work out the cost of one dishwasher load, including water if you pay a metered rate, rather than relying on a single average figure.

Quick answer

A cycle using 0.8kWh costs 21.1p in electricity at 26.32p/kWh. If it also uses 10 litres of water and your combined applicable metered water and wastewater charge is £4 per cubic metre, the water adds 4p. The example total is 25.1p, before detergent. Your actual bills and programme determine the cost.

Add metered water and wastewater

A cubic metre equals 1,000 litres. Use the applicable volumetric charges on your own water bill; wastewater billing may use a different volume or adjustment. If your bill is unmetered, an extra dishwasher cycle will not necessarily add a direct volumetric water charge.

Water cost in pounds = litres ÷ 1,000 × applicable combined charge per m³

For example, 10 litres at an illustrative £4/m³ adds £0.04. With 0.8kWh and a 15p detergent dose, the example total is 40.1p. At five cycles each week that is £104.15 over 52 weeks, assuming unchanged prices.

Eco vs intensive programmes

Eco settings can reduce energy consumption by using lower temperatures and longer washing times. Compare both programme energy and water figures from your own model. A longer programme does not automatically cost more.

Dishwasher vs washing by hand

Compare the same quantity of dishes and equivalent cleaning results. Hand-washing costs depend on the hot water volume, how it is heated, water charges and detergent. A gas-heated hot water system requires a different fuel rate and an allowance for boiler efficiency. An electricity-only dishwasher estimate cannot establish which whole task is cheaper.

Build your own weekly estimate

Record how many complete cycles you run in a typical week, multiply the total per-cycle cost by that number, then multiply by 52 for an annual estimate. Avoid running partially filled loads unnecessarily, while following your machine's loading instructions. Use the calculator for the electricity component and add your water and detergent separately.

Frequently asked questions

Does the calculator include water?

No. The linked calculator estimates electricity; this guide shows how to add metered water.

What does 80kWh per 100 cycles mean?

It is 0.8kWh per stated test cycle, costing about 21.1p at the reference electricity rate.

Is eco always cheapest?

Check the programme's actual electricity and water figures; cycle time alone is not enough.

Should I include my standing charge?

Not in the marginal cost of a cycle, unless your billing arrangement changes because of using it.

Methodology and further reading

The examples use transparent assumptions, not independently measured appliance or vehicle performance. Costs are calculated before rounding, and exclude standing charges unless explicitly stated. See Ofgem's unit rates, our energy-use guide and appliance cost comparison guide.
